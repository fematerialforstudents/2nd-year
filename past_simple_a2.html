Past Simple

Talk about yesterday, last week, last year. Learn regular verbs, beat the irregular ones, then face the boss level.

When do we use it?

For things that started and finished in the past. Look for time words:

yesterdaylast nightlast weekin 2019two days ago
Regular verb: verb + -ed  ·  same for I / you / he / she / it / we / they

✅ ❌ ❓ Sentence switcher

After did and didn't, the verb goes back to normal. No -ed!

🚨 Top 5 mistakes

Did you played? → Did you play?

She didn't liked it. → She didn't like it.

I studyed. → I studied.

We stoped. → We stopped.

He playd football. → He played football.

Irregular verbs break the rule

No -ed. You must learn them. Good news: they come in families, and the negative and question forms are exactly the same as for regular verbs (didn't / Did + base verb).

I went to Rome. → I didn't go to Rome. → Did you go to Rome?
